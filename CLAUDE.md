# psycho-mantis

Entry for the CIMC hackathon: a live webcam mirror that shows its prediction of the next frame,
drawn in Ghostty (`psychomanteum.py`), with Claude Code in the next pane closing the loop
(`mantis.py`, `/mantis` skill).

## Experiment log (standing task)

`EXPERIMENTS.md` is the record of everything we have measured. Keep it current:

- **Log every empirical result** - benchmarks, timings, skill/error numbers, parameter
  comparisons, calibrations - as a numbered entry (E1, E2, ...) with the date, the question, the
  setup (script/command, parameters, conditions), the raw numbers, the conclusion and caveats.
- **Log negative and inconclusive results too**, not only wins.
- **Back claims with entries.** Before stating that something is faster, better or works, check
  the log; cite the entry (e.g. "see E5"). A claim without an entry goes under "Open hypotheses"
  until it's tested.
- **Don't rewrite history.** If a later result contradicts an entry, add a new entry and a short
  note on the old one pointing to it.
